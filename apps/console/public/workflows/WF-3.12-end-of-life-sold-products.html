How the workflow works

Allocation

Each line is a waste stream with its treatment route; lines amalgamate to the category total. The factor depends on both the material and how it is treated, so the route is captured, not assumed.

Units & factors

The unit is mass — tonnes or kilograms. The factor is chosen by stream and treatment (landfill, recycling, composting, incineration, energy recovery).

Monthly breakdown

Where the source carries dates, the year splits into twelve period-aligned months, with copy-first-month-to-all; otherwise the annual figure stands. The split feeds the year-on-year quality-check.

Year on year

Every line is anchored on its source, so the same line reconciles across years and re-import does not duplicate. The line structure carries forward via the reporting template; only the year's values are entered. A line matching a known source maps automatically; a genuinely new one is confirmed and added to the catalogue.

Detail & quality-check

Lines amalgamate upward — entry to category, to scope, to total. Line-by-line detail opens in a drawer; in the quality-check each line sits beside its prior years, so an omission (last year a value, this year still zero) or a large swing is immediately visible.
