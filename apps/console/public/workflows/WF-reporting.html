Reporting — workflow

How a measured job becomes a validated, approved and published client report. The report always rests on locked job data; it locks only after both the client and NZI approve it.

The sections (shown or hidden per report)

  • Executive summary · Net-zero commitment · Background · Sites & boundary · Carbon emissions · Intensity metrics · Targets & pathway · Analysis by scope · Emissions by category · Carbon reduction plan · UK SRS readiness · Standards & methodology · Glossary · Full emissions · Emissions by site.
  • A required chart not in the job data → leave that section out, or add the data. A section only shows if its figures resolve from the locked data.

What stops a report publishing

  • QA — every row reviewed; calculations and quality tiers complete.
  • Behind the previous system — an imported job whose totals differ from V7's by more than 0.1 t in any scope can be composed but not published, until its rows are brought in. A two-year job needs a level drift check first.
  • A superseded snapshot — a bring-in after a snapshot marks it "earlier record of account · awaiting re-compose"; validate and publish refuse until the report is re-composed from the new snapshot.

Key points

  • Locked data is the single source of truth — figures and charts resolve from it; validation freezes them so the preview is exactly what issues.
  • Unlock is a normal, audited loop — the CRM (in the job, not the client in the portal) can unlock to correct; re-locking re-validates the report.
  • Two sign-offs to lock — the client approves on their side, then the CRM gives final approval; neither alone locks it.
  • The independent-approval gate is an organisation control: OFF now (one CRM prepares and final-approves), turned ON when NZI scales (a reviewer who isn't the preparer). Senior-only to change; every approval audited.
